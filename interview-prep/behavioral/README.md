# Behavioral Prep
## STAR Method
**S - Situation:** The context. Where were you, what the team was, what was at stake?
**T - Task:** What was specifically on _you_. Not what the team was doing - your responsibility or decision you owned.
**A - Action:** What did you do, step-by-step, in first person singular.
**R - Result:** What happened, quantified where possible, plus what you learned.
## Questions
- **Q:** What parts of OpenAI's mission statement resonate most to you?
- **Q:** Where do you see yourself in five years and how does OpenAI fit into that?
- **Q:** Why now?
- **Q:** Why not Anthropic / Deepmind / Meta?
- **Q:** What are your thoughts on AI safety and the risks of advanced AI systems?
- **Q:** What does responsible AI development mean to you in practice?
- **Q:** Tell me about a time you had to weigh a technical capability against potential harm?
- **Q:** Tell me about a time you made a mistake and how you handled it?
- **Q:** Tell me about a time you came up with a creative solution with incomplete data; a time where you took initiative on something that wasn't explicitly your responsibility; where you brought structure to an ambiguous situation; a time where you had to learn something new quickly with conflicting priorities.
	- **A:** Preflight / Faultline story
- **Q:** Tell me about a time where you and a cross-functional partner disagreed.
